# Roadmap

- Simpler everyday flow: a short Home, one Restore page, and a guided "Test a change".
- Measured results: before/after recordings with PresentMon, compared against run-to-run noise.
- Input devices: polling-rate support through the bundled, hash-pinned HIDUSBF package, with full undo.
- New tweaks are added only when they have a clear reason, a working undo and a way to check the effect.

Detailed planning notes are kept privately by the owner.
