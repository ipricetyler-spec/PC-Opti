# Marketing naming round — 2026-08-25

## Status

Owner decision recorded on 2026-08-25: **Dialed** is approved for the customer-visible product rebrand. This approval authorizes repository, executable, installer, shortcut, and current-documentation branding work. It does not claim comprehensive professional clearance, authorize public publication, or authorize acquiring domains and social accounts.

## Market position to name

The product is not merely a cleaner and should not be named like one. Its strongest consumer position is:

> Make the PC feel better for gaming and everyday work, show exactly what changed, and preserve a route back.

The brand therefore needs to communicate four ideas without sounding cautious or clinical:

1. Better real-world performance.
2. A simple, consumer-friendly experience.
3. Control over every applied change.
4. Credibility without vague boost claims.

The product descriptor can carry the category keywords (`Windows performance optimizer` or `gaming PC optimizer`). The brand itself should be short, memorable, pronounceable, visually clean, and broad enough for Gaming, Work / Creation, and Maintenance profiles.

## Product position

The product's defensible identity is its local evidence, action history,
verification, and supported rollback—not an absolute FPS promise. Naming and
customer-facing copy should describe those first-party qualities directly and
remain independent of other products or their claims.

This argues against another name built from `Opti`, `Boost`, `FPS`, or a hard-to-explain invented acronym. Those terms can remain in subtitles, search copy, and feature descriptions.

## Recommended shortlist

### 1. Dialed — marketing winner

- **Public descriptor:** `Dialed — Windows Performance Optimizer`
- **Gaming descriptor:** `Dialed — Gaming PC Optimizer`
- **Primary line:** `Your PC, dialed in.`
- **Supporting line:** `Optimize what matters. See every change. Keep a way back.`
- **Why it works:** Natural enthusiast language, immediate performance association, easy to say, and broad enough for games, work, and maintenance. It also creates useful calls to action: `Get Dialed`, `Run a Dialed scan`, and `Dial in this PC`.
- **Risk:** `Dialed` is a commonly used word. That raises clearance and search-distinctiveness risk even though the exact `Dialed PC` combination did not surface as a confusingly similar product in the preliminary search.
- **Domain observation:** `dialedpc.com` returned no Verisign `.com` RDAP registration record during the 2026-08-25 check. This is not a purchase guarantee or legal clearance.

### 2. Revertune — lower-collision backup

- **Public descriptor:** `Revertune — Windows Performance Optimizer`
- **Primary line:** `Tune forward. Keep the way back.`
- **Supporting line:** `Performance changes you can inspect, verify, and reverse.`
- **Why it works:** Encodes the product's strongest differentiator—optimization with a supported return path—while still sounding performance-oriented.
- **Risk:** More functional and less emotionally immediate than Dialed. Some users may initially read it as an audio product.
- **Domain observation:** `revertune.com` returned no Verisign `.com` RDAP registration record during the 2026-08-25 check. No obvious exact-name software collision appeared in the preliminary web search. Neither fact is legal clearance.

### 3. Mettle — premium performance territory

- **Public descriptor:** `Mettle — PC Performance Control`
- **Primary line:** `Put your PC to work.`
- **Supporting line:** `Measured tuning for games, work, and everything between.`
- **Why it works:** Short, confident, premium, and connected to performance under pressure without making an unsupported speed promise.
- **Risk:** The word is used by unrelated businesses and products, including a photography-equipment app, so discoverability and clearance may be harder.
- **Domain observation:** `mettlepc.com` returned no Verisign `.com` RDAP registration record during the 2026-08-25 check.

### 4. Headroom — enthusiast territory

- **Public descriptor:** `Headroom — PC Performance Control`
- **Primary line:** `Make room for what matters.`
- **Supporting line:** `Find the bottleneck. Reduce the noise. Keep control.`
- **Why it works:** A real performance concept understood by enthusiasts and applicable to gaming, streaming, and creation workloads.
- **Risk:** Highly common in audio and computing, and an existing PC game uses the name. It is the weakest clearance candidate in the shortlist.
- **Domain observation:** `headroompc.com` returned no Verisign `.com` RDAP registration record during the 2026-08-25 check.

### 5. Temper — controlled-performance territory

- **Public descriptor:** `Temper — Windows Performance Control`
- **Primary line:** `Performance, under control.`
- **Supporting line:** `Tune deliberately. Verify the result.`
- **Why it works:** The metaphor is controlled strengthening rather than reckless overclocking. It can support a restrained, premium visual identity.
- **Risk:** The metaphor needs explanation, the word has an anger association, and common-word clearance is likely difficult.
- **Domain observation:** `temperpc.com` returned no Verisign `.com` RDAP registration record during the 2026-08-25 check.

## Recommendation

Advance **Dialed** to a professional knockout search and retain **Revertune** only as the documented fallback:

- Choose **Dialed** if consumer appeal, word of mouth, and a strong gaming-first identity are the priority.
- Choose **Revertune** if distinctiveness and the reversible-performance promise are the priority.

The owner-approved commercial direction is **Dialed**, presented as `Dialed — Windows Performance Optimizer`, with `Your PC, dialed in.` as the lead line. Keep **Revertune** as the fallback only if professional clearance shows that Dialed is too crowded.

## Required clearance before renaming

Search federal, state, common-law, software, app-store, package-registry, domain, and social-handle use for the exact name and confusingly similar sound, spelling, meaning, and commercial impression. The USPTO explains why an exact-word web search alone is not comprehensive clearance: <https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching>.
